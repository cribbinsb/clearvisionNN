Three pictures of one moment
The original, the input as an ordinary bicubic enlargement (what a player would show without the network), and the network's output. In Magnify, press or drag in the top-left picture to choose the region.

Everything on this device
The camera simulation, the reduction and the motion search are Rust compiled to WebAssembly. An 8-bit model then runs entirely on the GPU, in the page's own WebGPU shaders, in integer arithmetic checked bit for bit against the Rust reference.

Float models
Their upscale, warp and blend stay in WebAssembly, and the network runs through ONNX Runtime Web on the chosen backend. The optional codec is the browser's own, through WebCodecs, or nanocodec.
